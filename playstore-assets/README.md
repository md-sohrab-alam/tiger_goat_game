|------|------|---------------------|

## Still needed from you (screenshots)

Play requires **at least 2 phone screenshots** (up to 8). Capture from the app:

1. Home screen  
2. Mid-game board  
3. AI / trays (optional)  
4. Tutorial (optional)  

Specs: JPEG or 24-bit PNG, phone portrait about **1080×1920** or device native.  
See also: `docs/SCREENSHOTS.md`

## Where to upload

Google Play Console → your app → **Grow → Store presence → Main store listing → Graphics**

1. App icon → `icon-512.png`  
2. Feature graphic → `feature-graphic-1024x500.png`  
3. Phone screenshots → your captures

# Privacy Policy — Bagh Bakri

**Last updated:** September 2026

Host this file on a public URL for Google Play (GitHub Pages, Firebase Hosting, or Notion).  
The same content is also shown in-app under **Privacy Policy**.

## Overview

Bagh Bakri is an offline board game. The core game (**2 Players · Offline** and **Play vs AI**) works without an internet connection and without creating an account.

## Data we collect

In the current version, the app does not require login and does not intentionally collect personal information such as name, email, or phone number for gameplay.

## Data stored on your device

The app may store simple preferences on your device (for example language, whether you have finished the tutorial, and gameplay settings such as move hints). This data stays on your phone and is not uploaded by us.

## Ads, analytics, and purchases

This version does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or in-app purchases. If we add them in a future update, this policy will be updated and the Play Store listing will describe any new data use.

## Children

Bagh Bakri is a strategy board game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the developer through the Google Play Store listing for Bagh Bakri, or open an issue on the project repository: https://github.com/md-sohrab-alam/tiger_goat_game
